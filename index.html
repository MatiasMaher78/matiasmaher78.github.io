About Me

I’m an automotive-focused Data Engineer building computer vision, OCR, ETL and workflow automation systems for real operational problems.

My work combines hands-on automotive knowledge — cataloguing, homologation, pricing and ERP workflows — with Python, SQL, YOLO, FastAPI, React and Power BI.

I’m building Autoparts Integrator, an automotive parts cataloguing pipeline that connects computer vision, OCR and human review, and developed Vehicle Data Print, a full-stack homologation tool validated with real users and vehicle data. I also maintain automotive data pipelines for OEM validation, pricing and business intelligence.

I’m self-taught and use LLMs as development accelerators for research, architecture and implementation, while keeping production workflows traceable and technically controlled.
