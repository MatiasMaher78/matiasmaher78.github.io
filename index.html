About Me

I’m a Data Engineer with an automotive background, focused on ETL pipelines, data quality, workflow automation and computer vision dataset engineering.

From November 2024 to October 2026 I worked at Reciclauto as a Data Engineer (Automation & AI Systems), where I built and ran a weekly OEM pricing ETL that replaced an outsourced process, a Playwright-based OEM reference validation tool and a Power BI data-quality dashboard. Before that, I spent two and a half years as an Automotive Homologation Technician at COC Europe.

Independently, I developed Vehicle Data Print, a full-stack homologation tool validated in daily use by homologation technicians, and I’m building Autoparts Integrator, a computer vision and human-in-the-loop cataloguing system with its own dataset governance layer.

I’m self-taught and use LLM assistants to speed up research, architecture and implementation, while keeping data workflows tested, traceable and under human control. More background on the About page.
